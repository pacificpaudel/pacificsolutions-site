Pricing

Transparent starting points. Every quote is still custom.

Pacific Solutions consults on a case-by-case basis — the figures below are honest starting indicators per practice, in EUR, excluding VAT. Your actual quote depends on scope, urgency and system complexity, and is always confirmed in writing before work begins.

How engagement types work everywhere on this page: Advisory is hourly, pay-as-you-go judgment with no delivery obligation. Project is a fixed-price, fixed-scope engagement defined in a written Statement of Work. Retainer is an ongoing monthly relationship for continuous support, monitoring, or fractional leadership.
