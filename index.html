Engagement model

Every project is scoped on a case-by-case basis

There is no one-size-fits-all package. We start with a conversation, agree on the smallest useful scope, and grow the engagement only as far as it needs to go — whether that's a two-hour advisory call or a multi-month platform build.

01

Discover

A free 30-minute call to understand your systems, constraints and goals.

02

03
