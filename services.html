Services

Six practices. Every engagement scoped to what you actually need.

Work with Pacific Solutions in whichever shape suits your problem: a single advisory session, a fixed-scope project, or an ongoing retainer. See indicative pricing for each practice on the pricing page.

01 / 06

Cloud & Infrastructure Architecture

Design and operate infrastructure that scales with the business instead of against it — whether you're leaving a data center or optimizing an existing multi-cloud footprint.

Discuss this practice

Cloud Migration & Strategy

Moving legacy workloads to AWS, GCP or Azure via rehosting, refactoring, or full re-architecting — matched to your risk tolerance and timeline.

Infrastructure as Code (IaC)

Automating environment provisioning with Terraform, OpenTofu, Ansible or CloudFormation, so environments are reproducible and reviewable.

Hybrid & Multi-Cloud Design

Unified operations across private data centers and multiple public cloud providers, with a single control plane for networking and identity.

Cloud Cost Optimization (FinOps)

Analyzing resource utilization, rightsizing instances, and establishing governance to cut cloud spend without cutting capability.

02 / 06

DevOps & Platform Engineering

Turn delivery into a repeatable, self-service process — so shipping software stops depending on one person's memory.

Discuss this practice

CI/CD Automation

Designing continuous integration and delivery pipelines (GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, Jenkins) that automate testing and release cycles.

Containerization & Orchestration

Packaging applications into Docker containers and running scalable clusters with Kubernetes, ECS, or OpenShift.

Internal Developer Platforms (IDP)

Building self-service tools and portals so developers can provision environments without filing operations tickets.

03 / 06

Custom Software Engineering & Modernization

Software that matches how the business actually works today — not the assumptions it was built on five years ago.

Discuss this practice

Legacy Application Modernization

Breaking monolithic software into microservices or serverless architectures, incrementally and without a "big bang" rewrite.

Custom Software Development

Designing and building full-stack applications, APIs, and backend microservices tailored to specific business needs.

Software Architecture Reviews

Assessing existing codebases, design patterns, and database models for performance, maintainability, and scalability bottlenecks.

04 / 06

Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) & Observability

See problems before your customers do, and recover faster when something still slips through.

Discuss this practice

Monitoring & Metrics

Implementing Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK/OpenSearch to track system health in real time.

Incident Management & SLA Design

Establishing automated alerting, runbooks, and incident response frameworks to reduce Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR).

Performance Tuning & Load Testing

Benchmarking infrastructure limits under heavy traffic and eliminating database or network latency.

05 / 06

Security, Compliance & Governance (DevSecOps)

Compliance treated as an architecture decision, not a document you write once a year.

Discuss this practice

Infrastructure Audits & Hardening

Scanning environments for misconfigurations, exposed endpoints, and compliance gaps.

Regulatory Compliance Readiness

Structuring infrastructure to meet frameworks like ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, or GDPR.

Identity & Access Management (IAM)

Setting up least-privilege policies, Zero Trust networking, and central identity management.

06 / 06

Strategic Technology Consulting

Independent, vendor-neutral advisory for decisions that are expensive to get wrong — delivered separately from any build work if you prefer a second opinion only.

Discuss this practice

Technical Due Diligence

Evaluating a target company's software stack, codebase quality, and infrastructure risk for M&A or venture funding.

Technology Roadmap & Vendor Evaluation

Recommending tech stacks, database engines, or third-party platforms based on long-term goals, not vendor incentives.

A note on scope: every practice above is available as standalone advisory (pay for judgment, not delivery), as a fixed-scope project, or as an ongoing retainer. Advisory-only engagements are common — you don't need to hire Pacific Solutions to build anything to get a second opinion.
